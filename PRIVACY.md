# Privacy Policy

Google Multi-Account Shield is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines our data handling practices.

## 100% Local Storage & Zero Telemetry Guarantee

This extension operates entirely locally within your browser:
1. **No External Servers**: We do not send your data, email addresses, profile pictures, or cookies to any external server.
2. **Zero Telemetry**: We do not track your usage, clicks, or any other telemetry data.
3. **Local Storage Only**: All account information (emails, names, avatars, and session cookies) is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
4. **Data Ownership**: You own your data. You can clear the vault and all stored information at any time.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue in this repository.
